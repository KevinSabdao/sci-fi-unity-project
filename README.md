# AR-1A
A fast-paced first-person shooter roguelike made in Unity where movement and constantly scavenging the environment (as well as enemies) are key to survival.

## Featuring aspects such as:
- Two weapons at once, which AR-1A can equip and swap around at will
- Free movement where AR-1A can run, slide and jump in any direction to explore areas as well as evade enemies
- An inventory system where you can collect scrap dropped by enemies which can be used to upgrade your weapons
- Explore a moody, gray facility populated by zombies and drones
- And a boss!

## How to play?
- Use WASD to move forward, backward, left and right, alongside your mouse to look around
- Spacebar to jump and Ctrl to crouch and/or slide
- Shift to run
- Left and right click fires your weapons in each hand
- E opens your inventory

## Where to download?
- On the main page click 'Releases'
- Download and extract from the .zip file
- Run "AR-1A.exe"
